Legend of Mana Native for Mac

It runs on Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer) with macOS 13 or newer. You also
need your own copy of Legend of Mana, the US version (SLUS-01013) or the
Japanese one (SLPS-02170), as a disc image or an extracted folder. No game
data is included.


Installing it

1. Drag Legend of Mana Native into the Applications folder.
2. Open it from Applications.

The first time, macOS won't open it. It says it can't verify that Legend of
Mana Native is free of malware. That's because the app isn't signed with an
Apple Developer ID. Sorry, I don't want to pay Apple $99 a year just to sign
an app at this time. Here's how to open it anyway. You only have to do this
once for each new version.

1. When macOS says it won't open it, click Done. Don't move it to the Trash.
2. Open System Settings and go to Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to Security. You'll see a line saying Legend of Mana Native
   was blocked. Click Open Anyway.
4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click Open Anyway again.

After that it opens normally.


If that doesn't work

If you don't see Open Anyway, or the launcher opens but the game won't start
when you press Play, you can do the same thing in Terminal. Open Terminal
(it's in Applications, in Utilities) and paste this:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Legend of Mana Native.app"

Press Return, then open Legend of Mana Native again. It tells macOS you trust
this app, and only this app.


The source is all on GitHub, if you want to see what you're running or build
it yourself: https://github.com/celophi/lom-native
